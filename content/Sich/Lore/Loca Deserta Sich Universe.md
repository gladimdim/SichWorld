Time of events: approximate beginning of the 17th century, 1674.

Political situation.

## Sich
Center of free Cossacks. Quite strong and stable country. Constantly raiding Crimea and Turkish coasts. In a state of constant war with the Turks. In friendly relations with the Hetmanate, but does not recognize its leadership or supremacy.

### Sich - Ottomans
State of constant war, mutual raids on each other. Sich Cossacks often unite with voluntary Cossacks from the Hetmanate and make campaigns against the Crimean Khanate and the Black Sea coast in Asia. Sich can repel a major war with the Turks only with the help of Sich and the Moscow Tsardom. Mutual rejection at a genetic level.

### Sich - Crimean Khanate
State of raiding war. Unfriendly relations, although trade contacts are maintained in the form of salt production, wine supplies, horses and livestock. Raids occur only for economic and material rewards. Without fanaticism and rejection of each other.

## Crimean Khanate
Dependent on the Ottomans. Military ally of the Turks. Constantly participating in mutual raids on Sich and the Hetmanate. Sometimes bribed by Poles to carry out attacks on Cossack lands. Wages war only at the expense of the Turks, who always help the Khanate. Cannot wage a long-term conflict on its own.

## Ottomans
A huge empire that wants to capture Podillia and generally destroy its rival in Eastern Europe - Sich. Carries out expansion into Eastern Europe, which puts it in a state of war with the Moscow Tsardom, the Hetmanate and Sich. Maintains neutrality with the Polish-Lithuanian Commonwealth, as the weakness of the Cossacks is beneficial to the Poles, who dream of restoring rule over Cossack lands.

## Hetmanate
An independent state that controls the Left Bank, part of the Dnieper region, Chyhyryn, Kaniv. Capital in Chyhyryn. As a result of internal strife, it has weakened slightly, but retains huge ideological influence on the population of Rus'. Has significant military power, but cannot compete with the Turks.

## Polish-Lithuanian Commonwealth
As a result of the formation of the Hetmanate, it significantly lost its position in Rus'. Kyiv is effectively a Hetmanate city. Has a strong army, but does not enter into open war with either Sich or the Hetmanate, although it constantly tries to weaken them through diplomacy and conspiracies.

## Moscow Tsardom
Takes an active part in the life of Sloboda Ukraine, which automatically extends its influence on the Hetmanate. Has a large army that can compete with the Turks on an equal footing. Maintains neutrality towards Sich and the Polish-Lithuanian Commonwealth, trying to increase influence on the Hetmanate through diplomacy.
