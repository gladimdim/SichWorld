## Mythical Component

In this world, at the level of everyday life, there are mavkas, devils, and other magical creatures. These creatures are very strongly tied to the locality.

For people, these are dangerous creatures that want to take their souls or get them lost, as in our folklore. Only strong personalities or tricksters can fight them off.

Creatures are encountered rarely, in areas uninhabited by people. People usually drive them away if they come too close, or when people capture new territories.

Forests, swamps, steppes, caves, rivers - all contain some mythical creatures that very often attack Cossacks.

Cossacks have a great fear of these creatures and do not like to fight them. Only by uniting into large groups do they drive them away. It is almost impossible to kill a mythical creature one-on-one.

## Household Magic

Cossacks have learned to brew various magical potions from pieces of mythical creatures and plants. This includes the ability to swim for a long time, restore life, see in the dark, upgrade their abilities and perks, and use some weapons for a certain time.

## Kharakterniks
Among the Cossacks, magical potions and communication or battles with mythical monsters are managed by kharakterniks. Each palanka has one kharaktirnik who helps all Cossacks with potions, allows them to unlock the possibilities of their brains so that they can take more advanced potions.
