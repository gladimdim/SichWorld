# General

The Christian religion has only recently begun to spread in the territories of the Zaporizhian Sich. The main power is held by priests who serve the old gods, very connected with local nature and mystical creatures. But they have no influence on external factors, countries, etc. They have their main strength from the lands of Sich, have almost no influence on the Hetmanate and Rus', although they once dominated there, but with the advent of Christianity, the influence of priests descended to Sich.

Each palanka has its own kharakterniks:

- [[Kharaktirnik Okun]] in Kodak
- [[Kharaktirnik Kniaz]] in Ingulsk

# Advantages

Provide access to communication with spirits, completing quests to strengthen ties with nature and spirits.
