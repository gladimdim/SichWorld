# General

The Christian religion has only recently begun to spread in the territories of the Zaporizhian Sich. The main influence comes from the Hetmanate and Rus'. Churchmen have administrative influence and connections with other states through the church, which gives them international influence and relations.

# Advantages

Provide access to money, completing quests to strengthen faith and eradicate witchcraft and priests.

## Spells
- [ ] Incense from evil spirits
- [ ] Amulet of saints (+ to player attributes)
- [ ] Holy water (healing)
- [ ] Weapon consecration (permanent weapon bonus)
- [ ] Prayer on relics (+ to player attributes until next prayer)

## General Quests
 - [ ] Return of faith attributes
 - [ ] Consecration of crosses on roads
 - [ ] Help the sick and holy fools
 - [ ] Light candles in chapels
 - [ ] Pray in churches
 - [ ] Search for faith artifacts
 - [ ] Find things of Andrew the First-Called
 - [ ] Delivery of holy relics to Sich
 - [ ] Sabotage old holidays (Ivan Kupala, Spas)
 - [ ] Finding and neutralizing witches
 - [ ] Reducing the influence of old priests
 - [ ] Blackening of witches and warlocks (plant corpses, forge evidence and frame a priest or witch)
