The territory of Sich is divided into palankas with centers in large town-villages of the palanka. In the game there are three palankas [[Sich/Lore/Sich/Palankas/Kodak Palanka|Kodak Palanka]] [[Ingul Palanka]], [[Sich/Lore/Sich/Palankas/Samara Palanka|Samara Palanka]]. Each palanka has its own specialization depending on natural conditions.

Once a year, Sich elects a Koshovy Otaman and the Sich Council. They determine all foreign policy of Sich, who to fight and when.

Also once a year, each palanka elects its own elders, who deal with administrative and economic management. They all report to the Sich Council. The Sich Council can set tasks for the elders to implement the decisions of the Koshovy or the Sich Council.

The Sich Council includes the Koshovy (executive position), scribe (office of the president), oboznyi (quartermaster), judge, osavul (Ministry of Internal Affairs).

Palankas were subordinate to the Sich Council. A palanka was commanded by a palanka colonel, who was elected from among the palanka elders. Palanka elders were elected for military exploits, authority, honesty and service to Sich. Anyone could nominate themselves for the position of elder, for this you need to be supported by a certain number of Cossacks.

Palankas are divided into kurins. Each kurin has its own kurin-hut right in Sich. But rarely anyone lives there, only during trips to Sich for reporting, exchanging goods with other palankas, etc. All Cossacks in the kurin lived and slept in one kurin. Married Cossacks have their own huts and live separately, but nearby.

Kurinny is an administrative position. Manages the life of his kurin. Sotniks are subordinate to him. And desiatniks are subordinate to them. This is the lowest governing unit in Sich.
