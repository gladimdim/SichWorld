## Features

Engaged in fishing and boat building. Everything related to the river and sea campaigns. Supply the Sich Army with boats, ships, pilots, equipment for military sea campaigns.

## Territory

Elongated along the Borysthenes territory, which is rich in small rivers flowing into the Dnieper. Generally lowland, many ravines where rivers flow, stone ridges sometimes protrude from the ground. Not suitable for agriculture. Between rivers and ridges are steppe and estuary.

## Military Affairs
Engaged in sabers. Manufactures good sabers, and the kurin also specializes in saber training. This palanka has the best masters of sabers. Resources for making sabers are exchanged with [[Sich/Lore/Sich/Palankas/Samara Palanka|Samara Palanka]].
