## Features

Engaged in horse and livestock breeding, skillfully handles the bow. Also skilled hunters. Supplies the best archers to the Sich Army.

## Territory

Large steppe between the Samara River in the south and Ingulets in the north. In the north there is also a large swamp. In the center there is a relic forest, and around is a steppe with gullies and ravines.

## Military Affairs
Engaged in archery. All masters are here. From their central forest they make good bows.
