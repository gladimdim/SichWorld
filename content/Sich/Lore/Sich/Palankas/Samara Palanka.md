## Features

Engaged in forests and extraction of minerals from the mountains. Manufactures muskets. Processes ore into metal. Industrial center of Sich.

## Territory

Located beyond the Samara River, which separates it from Kodak and Ingulsk. To the west are mountains where there is iron and coal. Also under the mountains are oak forests. The Samara River flows from a large lake in the north between the Samara and Ingul palankas.

## Military Affairs
Shoot well from muskets, which are also manufactured here. The best masters of firearms and warriors with muskets and cannons live here.
