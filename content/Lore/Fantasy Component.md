## Mythical Component

In this world, at the level of everyday life, there are mavkas, devils, and other magical creatures. These creatures are very strongly tied to the locality.

For humans, these are dangerous creatures that want to take their soul or lead them astray as in our folklore. Only strong personalities or tricksters can fight them off.

Creatures are encountered rarely, in areas uninhabited by humans. People usually chase them away if they come too close, or when people capture new territories.

Forests, swamps, steppes, caves, rivers - all contain some mythical creatures that very often attack Cossacks.

Cossacks have great fear of these creatures and do not like to fight them. Only by uniting in large groups do they drive them away. One on one, it is almost impossible to kill a mythical creature.

## Household Magic

Cossacks have learned to brew various magical potions from pieces of mythical creatures and plants. This includes the ability to swim for a long time, restore life, see in the dark, upgrade their abilities and perks, the ability to use some weapon for a certain time.

## Kharakternyks
Among Cossacks, magical potions and communication or battles with mythical monsters are handled by kharakternyks. Each palanka has one kharakternik who helps all Cossacks with potions, allows them to unlock the capabilities of their brain so they can take more advanced potions.
