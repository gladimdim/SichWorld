## Features

Engaged in fishing and boat building. Everything related to river and sea campaigns. Supply boats, ships, pilots, and equipment for military naval campaigns to the Sich Army.

## Territory

Elongated along the Borysfen territory, rich in small rivers flowing into the Dnipro. Generally lowland, many ravines where rivers flow, stone ridges sometimes protrude from the ground. Not suitable for agriculture. Between rivers and ridges are steppes and estuaries.

## Military Affairs
Specializes in sabers. Produces excellent sabers, and the kurin specializes in saber training. This palanka has the best saber masters. Resources for saber production are exchanged with [[Sich/Lore/Sich/Palankas/Samara Palanka|Samara Palanka]].
