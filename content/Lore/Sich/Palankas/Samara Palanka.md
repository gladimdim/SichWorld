## Features

Deals with forests and mining from the mountains. Manufactures firearms. Processes ore into metal. Industrial center of Sich.

## Territory

Located beyond the Samara River, which separates it from Kodak and Inhul. To the west are mountains with iron and coal. There are also oak forests under the mountains. The Samara River flows from a large lake in the north between Samara and Inhul palankas.

## Military Affairs
Shoot well with firearms, which they also manufacture. The best masters of firearms and warriors with guns and cannons live here.
