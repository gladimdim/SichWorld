## Features

Engaged in horse and cattle breeding, skillfully handles bows. Also skilled hunters. Supplies the best archers to the Sich Army.
## Territory

Large steppe between the Samara River in the south and Inhulec in the north. There is also a large swamp in the north. In the center there is a relic forest, and around it are steppes with gullies and ravines.

## Military Affairs
Specialize in archery. All are masters here. From their central forest they make excellent bows.
