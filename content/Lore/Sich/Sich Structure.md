The territory of Sich is divided into palankas with centers in large cities-villages of the palanka. In the game there are three palankas [[Kodak Palanka]] [[Inhul Palanka]], [[Samara Palanka]]. Each palanka has its own specialization depending on natural conditions.

Once a year, Sich elects a koshovyi otaman and the Sich Council. They determine all foreign policy of Sich, with whom to fight and when.

Also once a year, each palanka elects its own starshyna (elders), who deal with administrative and economic management. They all report to the Sich Council. The Sich Council can set tasks for the starshyna to implement decisions of the koshovyi or the Sich Council.

The Sich Council includes the koshovyi (executive position), clerk (president's office), oboznyi (quartermaster), judge, osavul (minister of internal affairs).

Palankas were subordinate to the Sich Council. The palanka was commanded by a palanka colonel, who was elected by the palanka starshyna among themselves. The palanka starshyna were elected for military achievements, authority, honesty and merits to Sich. Anyone could nominate themselves for the position of starshyna, for this they needed to be supported by a certain number of Cossacks.

Palankas are divided into kurins. Each kurin has its own kurin-hut right in Sich. But rarely does anyone live there, only during trips to Sich to report, exchange goods with other palankas, etc. All Cossacks in the kurin lived and slept in one kurin. Married Cossacks have their own huts and live separately, but nearby.

The kurin leader is an administrative position. Deals with the life of their kurin. Sotnyks (centurions) are subordinate to them. And desyatnyks (decurions) are subordinate to them. This is the lowest governing unit in Sich.
