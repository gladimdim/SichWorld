Time of events: conventional beginning of the 17th century, 1674.

Political situation.

## Sich
Center of free Cossackdom. Quite strong and stable country. They make constant raids on Crimea and Turkish shores. In a state of constant war with the Turks. In friendly relations with the Hetmanate, but does not recognize its leadership or primacy.

### Sich - Ottomans
State of constant war, mutual raids on each other. Sich Cossacks often unite with volunteer Cossacks from the Hetmanate and carry out campaigns against the Crimean Khanate and the Black Sea coast in Asia. Sich can repel a major war with the Turks only with the help of Sich and the Muscovite Tsardom. Mutual unacceptability at the genetic level.

### Sich - Crimean Khanate
State of raiding war. Unfriendly relations, although trade contacts are maintained in the form of salt production, supplies of wine, horses and cattle. Raids occur only for economic and material rewards. Without fanaticism and mutual rejection.

## Crimean Khanate
Dependent on the Ottomans. Military ally of the Turks. Constantly participate in mutual raids on Sich and the Hetmanate. Sometimes bribed by the Poles to carry out attacks on Cossack lands. Exports war only at the expense of the Turks, who always help the Khanate. By itself cannot conduct a long-term conflict.

## Ottomans
A huge empire that wants to capture Podillia and generally destroy its rival in Eastern Europe - Sich. Carries out expansion into Eastern Europe, which puts it in a state of war with the Muscovite Tsardom and the Hetmanate and Sich. Maintains neutrality with the Polish-Lithuanian Commonwealth, because the weakness of the Cossacks is beneficial to the Poles, who dream of restoring dominance over Cossack lands.

## Hetmanate
An independent state that controls the Left Bank, part of the Dnipro region, Chyhyryn, Kaniv. Capital in Chyhyryn. As a result of internal strife, it weakened a bit, but retains enormous ideological influence on the population of Rus'. Has significant military force, but cannot compete with the Turks.

## Polish-Lithuanian Commonwealth
As a result of the formation of the Hetmanate, significantly lost positions in Rus'. Kyiv is effectively a Hetman city. Has a strong army, but does not enter into open war with either Sich or the Hetmanate, although constantly tries to weaken them through diplomacy and conspiracies.

## Muscovite Tsardom
Takes an active part in the life of Sloboda Ukraine, which automatically extends its influence to the Hetmanate. Has a large army that can compete with the Turks on equal terms. Maintains neutrality towards Sich and the Polish-Lithuanian Commonwealth, trying to increase influence over the Hetmanate through diplomacy.
