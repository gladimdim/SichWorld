# Main

Christianity has only recently begun to spread in the territories of Zaporizhian Sich. The main power is held by priests who serve the old gods, very connected with local nature, mystical creatures. But they have no influence on external factors, countries, etc. The main strength comes from Sich lands, almost no influence on the Hetmanate and Rus', although they were once dominant there, but with the advent of Christianity, the priests' area descended to Sich.

Each palanka has its own kharakternyks:

- [[Kharakternik Okun]] in Kodak

# Advantages

Grant access to communication with spirits, completing quests to strengthen ties with nature, spirits.
