# Main

Christianity has only recently begun to spread in the territories of Zaporizhian Sich. The main influence comes from the Hetmanate and Rus'. Churchmen have administrative influence and connections with other states through the church, which gives them international influence and relations.

# Advantages

Grant access to money, completing quests to strengthen faith and eradicate witchcraft and priests.

## Castables
- [ ] Incense from evil spirits.
- [ ] Amulet of saints (+ to player's attributes)
- [ ] Holy water (healing)
- [ ] Consecration of weapons (permanent weapon bonus)
- [ ] Prayer on relics (+ to player's attributes until the next prayer)

 ## General Quests
 - [ ] Return of faith attributes.
 - [ ] Consecration of crosses on roads
 - [ ] Help the infirm and holy fools
 - [ ] Light candles in shrines
 - [ ] Pray in churches
 - [ ] Search for faith artifacts
 - [ ] Find things of Andrew the First-Called
 - [ ] Delivery of saints' relics to Sich
 - [ ] Sabotage old holidays (Ivan Kupala, Spas)
 - [ ] Finding and neutralization of witches.
 - [ ] Reducing the influence of old priests
 - [ ] Blackening witches and witchers (plant corpses, falsify and frame a priest or witch)
