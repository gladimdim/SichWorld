# Main

The Christian religion has only very recently begun to spread across the territory of the Zaporozhian Sich. The main influence comes from the Hetmanate and Rus. Churchmen have administrative influence and connections with other states through the church, giving them international reach and relationships.

# Advantages

They provide access to money and fulfillment of quests to strengthen the faith and eradicate witchcraft and pagan priests.

## Castables
- [ ] Incense against unclean forces.
- [ ] Amulet of saints (+ to player attributes)
- [ ] Holy water (healing)
- [ ] Weapon blessing (permanent weapon bonus)
- [ ] Prayer over relics (+ to player attributes until next prayer)

 ## General Quests
 - [ ] Restoration of faith attributes.
 - [ ] Consecration of roadside crosses
 - [ ] Help to the weak and infirm
 - [ ] Light candles in wayside shrines
 - [ ] Pray in churches
 - [ ] Search for artifacts of faith
 - [ ] Find the belongings of Andrew the First-Called
 - [ ] Deliver saints' relics to Sich
 - [ ] Sabotage of pagan festivals (Ivan Kupala, Spas)
 - [ ] Finding and neutralizing witches.
 - [ ] Reducing the influence of pagan priests
 - [ ] Discrediting witches and warlocks (planting corpses, framing a priest or witch)
