# Main

The Christian religion has only very recently begun to spread across the territory of the Zaporozhian Sich. The primary power is held by priests who serve the old gods, deeply connected to the local nature and mystical creatures. But they have no influence over external factors, other countries, etc. Their main strength comes from within the Sich lands; they have almost no influence on the Hetmanate and Rus, though they were once dominant there. With the arrival of Christianity, the domain of the priests shrank down to Sich.

Each palanka has its own charakternyky:

- [[Charakternyk Okun]] in Kodak
- [[Charakternyk Knyaz]] in Ingulsk

# Advantages

They provide access to communion with spirits, fulfillment of quests to strengthen connections with nature and spirits.
