Time of events: approximate early 17th century, year 1674.

Political situation.

## Sich
The center of free Cossackdom. A fairly strong and stable nation. They conduct constant raids on Crimea and the Turkish coastlines. In a state of perpetual war with the Turks. On friendly terms with the Hetmanate, but does not recognize its leadership or authority.

### Sich - Ottomans
A state of perpetual war, with mutual raids on each other. Sich Cossacks often unite with volunteer Cossacks from the Hetmanate and conduct campaigns against the Crimean Khanate and the Black Sea coast in Asia. Sich can repel a large war with the Turks only with the help of the Sich and the Muscovite Tsardom. Mutual rejection at a fundamental level.

### Sich - Crimean Khanate
A state of raiding warfare. Unfriendly relations, though trade contacts are maintained in the form of salt production, wine, horse, and cattle supplies. Raids occur only for economic and material gain. No fanaticism or mutual hatred.

## Crimean Khanate
Dependent on the Ottomans. A military ally of the Turks. Constantly participates in mutual raids on Sich and the Hetmanate. Sometimes bribed by the Poles to attack Cossack lands. Sustains warfare only through Turkish support. Cannot conduct a prolonged conflict on its own.

## Ottomans
A vast empire that wants to capture Podillia and generally destroy its rival in Eastern Europe — Sich. Expanding into Eastern Europe, placing it in a state of war with the Muscovite Tsardom, the Hetmanate, and Sich. Maintains neutrality with the Polish-Lithuanian Commonwealth (Rzeczpospolita), as the weakness of the Cossacks benefits the Poles who dream of restoring dominance over Cossack lands.

## Hetmanate
An independent state that controls the Left Bank, part of the Naddniprianshchyna, Chyhyryn, and Kaniv. Capital in Chyhyryn. Somewhat weakened as a result of internal strife, but retains enormous ideological influence over the population of Rus. Has significant military power but cannot match the Turks.

## Polish-Lithuanian Commonwealth (Rzeczpospolita)
Significantly lost its positions in Rus following the formation of the Hetmanate. Kyiv is effectively a Hetmanate city. Has a strong army but does not enter into open war with either Sich or the Hetmanate, though it constantly tries to weaken them through diplomacy and conspiracies.

## Muscovite Tsardom
Actively involved in the life of Slobozhan Ukraine, which automatically extends its influence to the Hetmanate. Has a large army that can match the Turks on equal terms. Maintains neutrality toward Sich and the Commonwealth, trying through diplomacy to increase its influence over the Hetmanate.
