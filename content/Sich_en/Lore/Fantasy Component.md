## Mythical Component

In this world, at the level of everyday life, there are mavky (forest spirits), chorty (demons), and other magical creatures from Ukrainian folklore. These creatures are very strongly tied to their localities.

For people, they are dangerous creatures that want to steal their souls or lead them astray, just as in folklore. Only strong individuals, or cunning ones, can fend them off.

The creatures are encountered rarely, in uninhabited areas away from people. People usually drive them away if they come too close, or when people take over new territories.

Forests, swamps, the steppe, caves, rivers — all contain mythical creatures that very often attack Cossacks.

Cossacks have a great fear of these creatures and dislike fighting them. Only by banding together in large groups do they drive them away. One-on-one it is nearly impossible to kill a mythical being.

## Folk Magic

Cossacks have learned to brew various magical potions from parts of mythical creatures and plants. This includes the ability to swim for a long time, restore health, see in the dark, boost one's abilities and perks, and gain skill with a particular weapon for a limited time.

## Charakternyky

Among Cossacks, the handling of magical drinks and dealings with mythical monsters are the domain of the charakternyky (warrior-shamans). Each palanka has its own charakternyk, who helps all Cossacks with potions, allowing them to unlock the abilities of their minds so they can take more advanced brews.
