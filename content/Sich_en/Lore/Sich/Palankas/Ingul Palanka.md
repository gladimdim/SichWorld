## Features

Specializes in horse and cattle breeding, skilled with the bow. Also expert hunters. Supplies the best archers to the Sich Army.

## Territory

A large steppe between the Samara River to the south and the Inhul River to the north. In the north there is also a large swamp. In the center is an ancient relict forest, surrounded by steppe with ravines and gullies.

## Military Affairs
Specializes in archery. All masters here. They craft excellent bows from their central forest.
