Deals in resources — transports timber. Expert marksmen with muskets. Supplies the best musket shooters and artillerymen to the army.

## Features

Specializes in forests and mining of minerals from the mountains. Manufactures muskets. Processes ore into metal. The industrial center of Sich.

## Territory

Located beyond the Samara River, which separates it from Kodak and Ingulsk. To the west are mountains with iron and coal deposits. Beneath the mountains are oak forests. The Samara River flows from a large lake in the north, between the Samara and Ingulsk palankas.

## Military Affairs
Expert marksmen with muskets, which they manufacture here. The best masters of firearms and warriors with muskets and cannons live here.
