## Features

Specializes in fishing and boat building. Everything connected with rivers and naval campaigns. Supplies boats, ships, pilots (lotsmany), and naval expedition equipment to the Sich Army.

## Territory

An elongated territory along the Borysfen (Dnipro) River, rich in small rivers flowing into the Dnipro. Generally lowland, with many small ravines where rivers flow, and stone ridges occasionally jutting out from the ground. Not suitable for farming. Between the rivers and ridges is steppe and estuaries.

## Military Affairs
Specializes in sabers. Produces excellent sabers, and the kurins specialize in saber training. This palanka has the best saber masters. Resources for saber-making are exchanged with [[Samara Palanka]].
