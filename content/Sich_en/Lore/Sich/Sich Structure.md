The territory of Sich is divided into palankas (districts) with centers in the major towns of each palanka. In the game there are three palankas: [[Kodak Palanka]], [[Ingul Palanka]], [[Samara Palanka]]. Each palanka has its own specialization depending on natural conditions.

Once a year Sich elects the Koshovy Ataman (commander-in-chief) and the Sich Council. They determine all foreign policy of Sich, with whom to fight and when.

Also once a year, each palanka elects its own elder council (starshyna), which handles administrative and economic management. They all report to the Sich Council. The Sich Council can assign tasks to the starshyna to carry out decisions of the Koshovy or the Sich Council.

The Sich Council includes the Koshovy (executive position), the Secretary (presidential office), the Quartermaster (supply officer), the Judge, and the Osavul (internal affairs).

The palankas were subordinate to the Sich Council. A palanka was commanded by the palanka colonel, elected from among the palanka starshyna. The palanka starshyna were chosen for military valor, authority, honesty, and service to Sich. Anyone could put themselves forward for a starshyna position, provided a certain number of Cossacks supported them.

Palankas are divided into kurins (companies). Each kurin has its own kurin-house right in Sich itself. But few actually live there — only during visits to Sich for reporting, trading goods with other palankas, etc. All Cossacks in a kurin lived and slept together in one kurin house. Married Cossacks have their own homes and live separately, but nearby.

The Kurinnyi (company commander) is an administrative position. He manages the life of his kurin. He is obeyed by the sotnyky (centurions). They in turn are obeyed by the desiatnyky (decurions). This is the lowest governing unit in Sich.
