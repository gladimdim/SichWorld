The charakternyky [[Charakternyk Okun]] can give approval to obtain the unique weapon — Sviatoslav's Saber.

The saber is golden and quite powerful.

When you complete quests from all charakternyky:

- [ ] [[Underwater Treasure Map]] from [[Charakternyk Okun]]
- [ ] [[Drive the Djinn from the Cave]] from [[Charakternyk Knyaz]]

They grant their consent that you are worthy of receiving the charakternyky relic. They ask you to approach the Stone Circle on the hill, where they proclaim you one of the worthy and present you with the Saber.
