The lighthouse is located north of Sich at the confluence of the Borysfen and Kodak rivers. It is not working because Ivan is afraid to return. Someone was climbing around the lighthouse at night and trying to get inside. So in the morning he fled and now sits in the bushes.

Ivan asks you to find out what was going on and whether he can return.

The unnamed hero must approach the lighthouse. There they will see tracks on the ground. Following them leads the player to the bushes by the river, where three Tatars are hiding. If you deal with them, one of them will have a note from Kyiv. A Polish nobleman is paying them to break the lighthouse or kill the lighthouse keeper.

The player returns to Ivan Svitilo. Two options: tell the full truth including that someone wants to kill Ivan, or just say you killed some Tatars — probably ordinary bandits.

The first option gives less experience and no money. The second gives more experience and 100 hryvnias, because Ivan will be relieved that everything passed and will no longer be afraid to stay at the lighthouse.
