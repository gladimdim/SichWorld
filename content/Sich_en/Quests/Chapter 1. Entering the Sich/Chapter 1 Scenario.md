# ❓ Chapter 1

You disembark from a boat onto the shore, where you are met by other survivors and Cossacks. Ostap comes up and asks if everything is alright, since he slept through the whole journey back. A dialogue recounts what happened, you also learn about the successful raid on Kaffa and that many prisoners were freed. You also ask how to get to the Ataman, but they laugh at you and say he doesn't receive people off the street.

You also cannot enter Sich, because you are not a Cossack — you must first join one of the palankas (three to choose from). To join a palanka, you must prove yourself capable in both warfare and household matters.
