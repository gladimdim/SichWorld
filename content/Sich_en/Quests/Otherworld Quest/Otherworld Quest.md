# ❓ Otherworld Adventure

The hero finds themselves on the shore of an island shrouded in mist, without weapons or possessions. In the distance [[Mavky]] are visible. On the shore is a wrecked boat where a bow or saber can be found, depending on proficiency.

On the island there is a reed-covered shore, a dark forest, a swamp, an old cemetery, and the ruins of a fortification.

In the forest by a campfire, Yakym Pohrebnyi [[Yakym Pohrebnyi]] is spending the night — a Cossack who has lost his horse.

In the swamp there is the ghost of a horse, and the [[Vodianyi]] Water Spirit Kurylo [[Vodianyi Kurylo]] guards it.

In the cemetery there is a fresh grave with a cross bearing the inscription "Pohrebnyi."

Among the fortress ruins is a kurin (hut) where old grandpa Blahomir [[Blahomir]] lives.

In all locations there are many weak mobs of the appropriate type (in the forest — wolves, in the swamp — various unclean spirits, etc.)
