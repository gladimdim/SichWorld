# ❓ Find Yakym Pohrebnyi's Horse

[[Yakym Pohrebnyi]] tells you his horse ran away and got lost somewhere in the swamp. He asks you to find it.

In the swamp you can find the ghost of the horse. Approaching it is prevented by [[Vodianyi Kurylo]]. You can fight and kill him or listen and talk it through. Then he will explain that Yakym is dead but refuses to accept it, and you need to persuade him to accept this. For this purpose he suggests going to the cemetery and finding Yakym's grave. (The hero may have already found it in the quest [[Escape from the Island]]).

Pohrebnyi listens to the story and asks to be taken to the horse. On the way they clear mobs together. There he stands silently for a while, then says yes, this is his horse, and he feels something. Then he needs to be taken to the grave, where he finally admits he is dead. If by this time you received the task to bring him to [[Blahomir]], you can take him; if not, this quest ends here.
