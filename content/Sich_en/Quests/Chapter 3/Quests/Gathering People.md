[[Mykola Pysarenko]] gives you the quest to gather people for the campaign from each palanka. He gives you a list of persons who must arrive at the assembly.

The list contains various people whom you must find and confirm they will carry out the secretary's orders.

The orders are:
 - [ ] Arrive at Sich for weapon procurement
 - [ ] Gather herbs for hospitals after the return
 - [ ] Count the number of Cossacks in the palanka ready for the campaign
 - [ ] Launch disinformation through [[Ales Lytvyniv]] that the Cossacks are not planning a campaign
