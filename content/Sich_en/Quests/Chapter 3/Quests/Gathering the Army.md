[[Ivan Sirko. Ataman.]] gives you the quest to gather supplies and troops for the campaign against the Turks at Ochakiv.

For this you need to visit all the palankas, give them their assignments and ensure their execution.

He will give you a letter to [[Ivan Mashna. Quartermaster]] so that he begins gathering provisions, horses, and other supplies. This is a sub-quest.

[[Mykola Pysarenko]] gives the quest [[Gathering People]].

Upon completing both these quests, return to the Ataman with a report.
