In a small cottage you find a blind Cossack. He asks you to bring him berries from the forest because he himself once got lost in the forest and barely found his way out. His kurin feeds him, but he very much wants to make jam.

Behind the cottage you can pick berries. You need to collect 10 of one type. But if you give him an additional 10 of another type of berries, he will increase the reward.

Simple reward: XP + money.

Larger reward: 2x XP + a rare saber that he found in a nearby cave.
