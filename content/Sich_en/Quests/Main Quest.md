# ❓ Main Quest

[[Chapter 0]]

[[Chapter 1. Entering the Sich]]


Sitting in Kaffa, a man who has been beaten to death and is dying begins to speak with you. He says Cossacks will attack soon and you will have a chance to escape, as the attack will be unexpected. But he is running out of time and reveals that he is Paturnakh — he was captured as a teenager and was to be sold, but he accepted Islam and thus saved himself. He started working in Kaffa and eventually achieved a certain position in the slave market. But he always remained loyal to Sich.

Through envoys he became an agent of the Ataman, helping exchange prisoners from both sides. But a month ago he learned a military secret: the Turks have decided to launch a massive raid on the lands of the Zaporozhian Host. For this they have already assembled supplies in Ochakiv, waiting only for spring weather to transport troops by sea and attack Sich at the start of the warm season.

The military operation is being kept secret. He sent a message to the Ataman, but the messenger was intercepted and he himself was captured. Nearly beaten to death, today is the day of execution. He passes the hero a ring and asks you to reach Sich and warn the Ataman.

If he gives you this ring, the Ataman will understand you are telling the truth. But only he will recognize it. Best not to talk to other Cossacks about this secret, as they might run to the Turks for reward and reveal that their raid has been discovered.

At this moment a wall with a door is blown apart by a cannonball, killing the prisoner.

[[Chapter 0]] begins.

Task for the hero: reach Sich, get inside, and gain an audience with the Ataman.

To enter Sich, you must become a Cossack of one of the palankas: [[📃Ingulsk Introduction]] [[Main Quest Line]] [[Joining the Samara Palanka]]

To get to the Ataman, you need to complete two quests of your choice // TODO

During the audience the Ataman recognizes the ring and listens to you. He understands this is a great opportunity to strike preemptively and capture the Turks' entire army supply. But for that the secret must be kept for now. He asks you to begin preparations for the raid, requiring preparatory quests:

// TODO
- Gather horses
- Build boats
- Gather provisions
- Gather gunpowder, bullets, and arrows
- Gather cannons

The hero completes all these quests by talking with the palanka atamans.
When the preparations are complete, the raid can begin.

Before the raid begins, the hero can choose 3-4 companions to help, as things will get intense.

The raid approaches Ochakiv. To capture it, you must pass through underwater and underground caves and plant explosives.

To enter the caves, you must defeat Turkish guards and various tough mobs inside.

After this, the hero returns to the siege camp and gives the signal to detonate the wall. It collapses and allows the Cossacks to begin the assault. The hero charges in personally; the first objective — capture the wall and not let it be retaken.

After that, the interior of Ochakiv must be cleared.

Then comes the assault on the Citadel with the cannons the hero gathered in the quest. The doors are blasted open and the hero fights the commandant of Ochakiv one-on-one.

After that, cuts off his head and places it on a pike.

The end.

A cutscene shows Cossacks gathering all the provisions, weapons, cannons, and wagons, then setting off for home.

Curtain.
