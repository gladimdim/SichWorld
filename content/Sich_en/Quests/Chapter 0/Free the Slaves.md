# ❓ Free the Prisoners

During the bombardment a fire started; there is only time to choose one room to unlock in time. You choose to unlock the shackles of one of three prisoners.

This will later affect [[Chapter 1. Entering the Sich]] — the first assistance upon return. Depending on the room chosen, you will receive: a little money from the prisoner in the first room, a saber from the second, a bow from the third.
