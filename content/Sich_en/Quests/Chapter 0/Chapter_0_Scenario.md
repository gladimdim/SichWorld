# Chapter 0
The scene begins in a room where men are sitting. One of them is our hero. Another badly beaten prisoner is thrown in.

Having caught his breath a little, he begins to speak with you.

I am to be executed soon, but I must pass information about the Turks' plans to someone. I am Paturnakh — for more than 5 years I have been monitoring the Turks here, reporting back to Sich when possible. But I was uncovered and now they will execute me. Listen carefully: the Turks have prepared a massive supply caravan, already ready and standing in Ochakiv. They are waiting only for spring to transport 10,000 troops by sea and strike Sich. This will be a catastrophe. Here, take this ring — with it Sulima will believe you, as this is his ring. Your task is to reach Sich and inform Sulima — he is the Koshovy (Ataman). But tell this information only to him! Among the Cossacks there will be those who will use it for their own ends, and that cannot be allowed. Remember, only until spring remains, you must warn the Koshovy so he can take measures — he knows better. By the way, what is your name? I want to know the name of the one who will save our state.

My name: ""

At this point guards enter, take Paturnakh outside, and strangle him in the courtyard.

At the same moment an explosion occurs, debris flies everywhere, everyone starts screaming, Turks start running somewhere. This is Sulima's attack on Kaffa! A chance to escape. One of the cannonballs blows open the doors and kills the guards. The hero takes a saber and starts fighting out to the yard. A tutorial begins on how to do things, a duel with a Turk. Then you pick up a bow or musket and kill another Turk. A fire starts, other prisoners ask you to unlock their shackles, but out of 3 prisoners only one set of shackles (time-limited). [[Free the Slaves]]. After this you run outside and Cossacks rescue you. Others perish.
