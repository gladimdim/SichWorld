# ❓ Chapter 2

After joining any of the palankas, the hero can enter [[../../../Січ/Січ]] territory.

In the Outer Circle of Sich you can ask where to find the Ataman. He is in the Inner Circle (IC), which is guarded by Cossacks and cannot be entered casually.

At the gates of the IC stands the Quartermaster [[Ivan Mashna. Quartermaster]]. He is inspecting cannons after the campaign, which are being prepared for storage.

He issues the quest for access to the IC: [[Entering the Higher Circle of Sich]]
