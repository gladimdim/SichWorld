You learn at Sich that the secretary [[Mykola Pysarenko]]'s son has fallen ill. You need to cure him.

You can ask the apothecary here about medicine — the one where [[Mykola Pysarenko]] is hanging around. But he only deals in European remedies and the next shipment will be who knows when, because the lighthouse isn't working and boats barely come. Plus there's an embargo on trade with the Commonwealth.

You ask if medicine can be found somewhere in Zaporizhia. He says he heard that palankas have their own doctors who wander around various places and often find very rare plants. You could look there.

You need to find the doctor [[🧑 💵Pechyborshch]]. He says he can make a potion only if you find a fern flower. Such flowers can grow at night near the lair of [[Chorty]] in swampy forests. You need to kill a chort and in its nest a herb will be growing, which will work for the potion. You brew the potion, bring it to Sich, and the secretary's son is cured.

The herb is only there at night.

You bring the herb to [[🧑 💵Pechyborshch]], who immediately brews the potion and gives it to you.

You bring the potion to [[Mykola Pysarenko]].

You can sell the potion for 1000 hryvnias, as his son's life is at stake. But then he will not vouch for you in the quest [[Merchants from Lithuania]] when you bring in contraband.

If you simply give him the potion, he will pay you 50 hryvnias as a formality. And later he will vouch for you in the quest [[Merchants from Lithuania]].

In any case, he will grant permission to enter the Higher Circle of Sich.
