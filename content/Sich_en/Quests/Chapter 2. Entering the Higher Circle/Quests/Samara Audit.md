You need to find out how much ore and coal the Cossacks of the Samara Palanka have mined.

You need to speak with [[Turchynov Samara Kurinnyi]]. He will grant permission to talk with other Cossacks, as he needs to submit his own report.

You go west to the mountains, and at the miners' camp you ask how much coal and iron have been extracted. But they cannot tell you because their ataman [[Andrii Krotulo]] went very deep into the mine, as they found a new cave. But for two days no one has been in contact, as mole-rats got into the cave and are keeping other miners out.

You descend into the mine, asking for directions. You go deep into the mine, deal with the mole-rats, and find the injured [[Andrii Krotulo]] and his companions. They were poisoned by carbon monoxide and are simply lying on boards up above. They climbed up there hiding from the mole-rats. You need to give each of them a health potion and they will recover. Krotulo will tell you how much coal and iron they will deliver this time.

[[Andrii Krotulo]] will complain that they would have extracted more but are given too few tools. So he asks you to pass this on to the Ataman [[Ivan Sirko. Ataman.]], launching the quest [[Tools for Miners]].

After this you go to [[Turchynov Samara Kurinnyi]] and he writes you a document with the audit. With it you can return to [[Ivan Mashna. Quartermaster]], who will give a lot of XP for this.
