#chapter2
Issued by [[Ivan Mashna. Quartermaster]] for entry into the [[Higher Council of Sich]]. He stands in the warehouse and reviews goods.

[[Ivan Mashna. Quartermaster]] says the kurinnyi are lying to him and not showing the true state of the palankas. So he asks you, as a newcomer, to visit all 3 palankas and find out the real situation regarding provisions, weapons, and other matters.

The quest adds three sub-quests: [[Kodak Audit]] [[Samara Audit]]

Before submitting any report, you can stop at the tavern, and [[Zhydov]] can tell you whether there is truth in it. The innkeeper will always answer for 50 hryvnias. With this true/false information you can go to [[Ivan Mashna. Quartermaster]]. In the dialogue with him, if you found out the truth from the innkeeper, a reply option about accuracy will appear. Otherwise you will speak at random.

But if you tell him the real truth from [[Zhydov]], he will always give money + XP. Otherwise just XP.
