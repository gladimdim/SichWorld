#chapter2

The quest is issued by [[Ivan Mashna. Quartermaster]] as a sub-quest within [[True Audit]] for entry into the [[Higher Council of Sich]].

You need to question the Cossacks in [[Kodak]] palanka about the state of supplies. You can ask [[Ostap. Kodak Kurinnyi]]. He will play dumb and say he reports honest figures.

Then you can go to the blacksmith [[Kovalenko]] to find out about the status of saber deliveries. He will give you a complete list of completed work if you completed his quest [[Blacksmith's Coal]].

Other information will be given to you by [[Pylyp the Fisherman in Kodak]] if you did his quest [[Torn Nets]]. He will say that fish stocks were indeed low because his nets were being torn. And he will give you an accurate, honest report.

With these reports you can return to [[Ivan Mashna. Quartermaster]] and close the report for this palanka.
