[[Charakternyk Knyaz]], when you ask if you can help him in the palanka, says that lately the unclean forces have been bothering his palanka less because they seem to have internal conflicts or troubles. He thinks it might be a trap and asks you to go with him to the caves. You meet him near the cave, as agreed, and he says he just spoke with the chief Chort (demon), who did not attack him but instead offered a truce and mutual assistance. The problem is that a pack of shaitans has settled in another cave — many of them, and the chorty cannot defeat them. So they ask you to drive the foreign intruders back to the Ottomans, because if the shaitans come, djinns or even Iblis himself might follow, and then things will be bad for everyone.

You go to the other cave, enter it, and kill all the shaitans. The shaitans had been rummaging through the chorty's stolen human treasures. There you find a lot of money, and also an envelope with a note and a lock of hair. This launches the quest [[Devil's Popaddya]].

After that you exit the cave. [[Charakternyk Knyaz]] says he will speak with the chief Chort at night. After a day, in conversation, you can ask about the outcome. Knyaz will say the chorty thank you and as a reward say that someday they will repay you when needed.

The quest gives a lot of XP. And [[Charakternyk Knyaz]] pays you money.

If in the conversation you tell him about [[Devil's Popaddya]] and the curse, he will laugh that a priest's wife is consorting with demons and that the church is full of such two-faced people. And he will give you more XP.
