
# Hero Attributes

## Strength
Affects what melee weapons you can equip.
Affects what muskets you can carry.
Affects damage.

## Agility
Affects what bows you can take.
Affects damage with bows and muskets.

## Intelligence
Affects what potions you can brew.
Allows reading various books that can improve hero attributes.

## Health
Determines the hero's health points. Increases with each level. Potions can also be brewed or purchased to increase health.

# Hero Experience Points
The hero gains experience points by killing NPCs, completing quests, etc. When a certain number of points is reached, the hero gains a new level. Experience points can only increase.

# Hero Training Points
With each level gained, the hero receives 10 training points that can be spent at trainers to improve weapon mastery, strength, agility, etc. Points do not regenerate. Only gaining a level grants +10 each time.

# Hero Level
Experience points, when reaching set thresholds, grant the hero a level. Each level gives training points.
