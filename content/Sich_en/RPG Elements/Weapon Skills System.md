Each weapon requires specific hero attribute values to equip — such as Strength, Agility, or Intelligence. If the player lacks the required attributes, they cannot use that weapon.

The player starts with zero skill in all weapons.

The player can exchange training points at trainers for weapon proficiency.

Skill level affects critical damage.

| Skill % | Name        | Effect                                          |
| -------- | ----------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| 0 - 29   | Beginner    | 1 strike without combo, slow strikes.           |
| 30-59    | Fighter     | 2-3 strike combo, fast strikes                  |
| 60-89    | Master      | Even faster strikes and combos of 4-5 strikes   |
| 90-100   | Expert      | Phenomenally fast strikes                       |

## Damage Calculator

Critical hit chance equals the skill level for that weapon.

Critical hit formula:
(weapon_damage + weapon_skill) - armor

Normal hit formula:
(weapon_damage + weapon_skill) / 2 - armor

Standard damage in any case is 2-3 hp.
