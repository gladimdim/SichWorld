Dmytro Poroshenko is a messenger from the Hetmanate to the [[Ottoman Sultanate]].

He has been at Sich for several weeks and is preparing to travel on, but at the [[Tavern]] he was robbed of the gifts meant for the Sultan. He fears admitting he was drinking in the tavern, so he asks the hero to either find his stolen gifts or find some other gift for the Sultan — something very valuable or special.

The hero goes to the [[Tavern]] and can ask [[Zhydov]] for money to find out what happened. It turns out the messenger lost a set of rings with precious stones at cards. You can ask the local Cossacks drinking there who won those rings. They are all slippery types and won't admit anything, but one hints that for 10,000 hryvnias he can "find" the rings and return them. If the hero pays, the Cossack returns the rings. With them you can return to [[Dmytro Poroshenko (Messenger)]], who will be overjoyed and give XP. He also promises to mention you when you return to the Hetmanate, and gives you an agility potion.

Alternatively, you can go to [[Charakternyk Knyaz]] and ask him about special gifts for distinguished persons in the [[Ottoman Sultanate]], whether he knows what that might be.

If we completed the quest [[Drive the Djinn from the Cave]], he will mention it — specifically that djinns were defeated there, and they may have had some artifacts. He can speak with the chorty (demons) to have them reward you in this way. In the morning he gives you a golden djinn lamp that the chorty captured. Such lamps are very rare and are usually put on display as a symbol that one has overcome the unclean and is not afraid of it. Take the lamp to [[Dmytro Poroshenko (Messenger)]], who crosses himself in fright and says what devilry you brought. You explain that it is a symbol of strength and bravery in the Sultanate and the Sultan would be glad to display such a lamp. For this gift he gives you a potion of permanent strength.

If the quest [[Drive the Djinn from the Cave]] has not been completed yet, [[Charakternyk Knyaz]] will say that sometimes djinns and shaitans wander into their territory, and he also contests them and sometimes takes various loot, which he later sells. So you can find a cave with djinns somewhere and search there. And he happens to have just such a quest [[Drive the Djinn from the Cave]] where you might find something. You need to complete the quest to get the djinn lamp and bring it to [[Dmytro Poroshenko (Messenger)]].
