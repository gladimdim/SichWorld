[[Ustyna, the Baker's Daughter]]'s father [[Karpo Kalach]] is against the marriage with [[Oles Znaidevodа]].

The father is against it because who will take care of the bakery — he has only one daughter. And this [[Oles Znaidevodа]] is constantly sailing on rivers and won't have time for it. The father says the daughter won't be able to manage the business alone because she doesn't bake bread very well.

[[Ustyna, the Baker's Daughter]] gives you a tip to find her some super-pastry recipe to prove to her father she can run the business. This recipe you can buy from [[Ales Lytvyniv]], who will sell you a Charlotte cake recipe.

To prove that [[Oles Znaidevodа]] will be useful. You need to talk with him and explain the father's position. As a pilot he can buy all kinds of spices from Crimea very cheaply, and in this way [[Karpo Kalach]]'s baked goods will actually become more valuable.

With these arguments you go to [[Karpo Kalach]] and he agrees to the marriage.

And [[Oles Znaidevodа]] will smuggle the [[Merchants from Lithuania]] goods for free and also give you a talisman.
