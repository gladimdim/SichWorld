In the marketplace at Sich you are approached by a merchant from Lithuania, [[Ales Lytvyniv]], who cannot deliver his goods from Kyiv because no one is allowed through the rapids as pilots are being denied. He asks you to find him a pilot and promises a reward once his boats arrive at Sich.

First you need to find the pilot [[Ivan Shukai Vodu]] or [[Oles Znaidevodа]], who will agree to help you. They are reluctant to take the job because an unannounced order from the Kish Council has come down not to let boats through, fearing that spies and scouts from the Commonwealth might slip in. So only their own boats are being let through.

Both pilots, when they agree, say that without the lighthouse on the river they won't be able to navigate the boats. So you need to go north and restart the lighthouse in the quest [[Ivan Svitilo's Lighthouse]]. After the lighthouse is restarted, the pilots agree to navigate the boats.

And after a few days new boats appear at the dock.

But you are no longer allowed into the Higher Council of Sich, because the contraband quest begins.
