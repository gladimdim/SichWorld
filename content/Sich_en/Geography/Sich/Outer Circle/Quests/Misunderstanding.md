After completing the quest [[Merchants from Lithuania]], the secretary [[Mykola Pysarenko]] and the quartermaster [[Ivan Mashna. Quartermaster]] forbid entry to the Higher Council, because you brought contraband.

The secretary, if you correctly completed the quest [[The Secretary's Sick Son]], will vouch for you.

The quartermaster gives you the quest [[Gifts for the Bride]], to find something for his daughter's wedding.

Either of them, if you have met their condition, lets you back into the Higher Council.
