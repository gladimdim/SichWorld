[[Ivan Svitilo]], if you encounter him near the lighthouse, will tell you the lighthouse isn't working because someone has been climbing up to it every night and banging on the door. He was very frightened and fled, and now sits in a small hut here because it's safe and no one attacks. He asks you to deal with the problem.

Indeed, scratches are visible on the lighthouse door, but it wasn't an animal or a demon — the marks are from axe blows and a mace. You are given a clue that whoever did this is a human. You walk around the lighthouse and spot horse tracks. Following them through a small grove near the river, you come upon Tatars. You kill 2-3 of them. One of them carries a letter from a Polish nobleman, who is paying them to sabotage the lighthouse to halt river traffic to Sich.

You go to [[Ivan Svitilo]] and tell him it wasn't demons, but people. He returns to the lighthouse and lights it.

If you later mention the lighthouse and the note to the Ataman [[Ivan Sirko. Ataman.]], he will give a lot of XP and send a guard for [[Ivan Svitilo]].

After that, [[Ivan Svitilo]] will give you some gear, armor, or weapon that he found on an abandoned boat.
