Wife of [[Pip Prokip]].

Bewitched the hair of [[Little Cook]], so that she would meet with [[Pip Prokip]] less. The resolution of this story is in quest [[Devil's Popaddya]]. Dislikes [[Little Cook]], because her husband constantly goes to eat there, and she thinks it's not just eating.

The envelope with hair and a note to the chorty can be found in [[Drive the Djinn from the Cave]].
