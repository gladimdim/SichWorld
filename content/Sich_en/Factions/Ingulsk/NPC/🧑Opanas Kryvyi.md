# 🧑 Opanas Kryvyi

#npc

## Who
Hunter of the Ingulsk Palanka. One of the best marksmen — quiet, patient, can wait for prey for hours. Got his nickname from a broken nose, which healed crooked after a fight in his youth.

## Character
Calm on the outside, but stubborn to the point of insanity. If he decided he's right — you can't budge him with word or fist. Speaks slowly, weighing each word. Considers himself smarter than most Cossacks around, and sometimes this shows in his tone — which irritates others. Not a coward, but avoids direct confrontations, thinking a smart hunter doesn't fight without need.

## Appearance
Thin, sinewy, medium height. Nose broken and healed sideways — recognizable from a distance. Eyes narrow, attentive, constantly studying something. Moves quietly, even when not hunting. Dressed in a worn leather zhupan (coat), good boots. Bow always on his back, quiver on his side. Sits by the fire and pointedly sharpens his knife when angry.

## Role
Usually keeps to the edge of the palanka or goes hunting. When in the palanka — sits by his fire, drying meat.

## Connections
- [[🧑Khoma Riznyk]] — quarreled over the catch. Used to hunt together, as they complemented each other well: Opanas shot, Khoma skinned.
- [[💵🧑Honta]] — respects as a trainer, sometimes consults about bows
- [[🧑Naum Lysenko. Ingulsk Kurinnyi]] — obeys him, but thinks the kurinnyi should have sorted it out himself, not sent strangers
