# 🧑 Taras Chub

#npc

## Who
A herdsman who grazes the palanka's horses and livestock on the steppe meadows. Responsible and reliable, but closed off.

## Character
Silent, gloomy. Dislikes strangers, dislikes idle talk. Speaks briefly and to the point. Respects only those who prove themselves by deed, not word. But if you earn his trust — he'll give valuable advice without extra words.

## Appearance
Stocky, sun-tanned, hands like shovels. Always with a whip and a knife on his belt. Dressed plainly, without adornment.

## Role
Stands by the pen at the edge of the palanka. Knows the steppe like the back of his hand — what runs where, who hides where.

## Connections
- [[🧑Naum Lysenko. Ingulsk Kurinnyi]] — subordinate to him, respects him, but thinks the Kurinnyi underestimates the threat from the steppe.
- [[💵🧑Honta]] — normal relations; Honta sometimes helps drive predators away from the herd.
