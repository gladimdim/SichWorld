Covers the needs of [[Ingulsk]] palanka, fights chorty, lishyi, and other unclean creatures in the forest, also protects the horse herds from attacks by supernatural beings.

The charakternyk has lived in this palanka for three hundred years and has seen much. One of the Cossacks quarreled with him in the tavern and stole his enchanted saber and took it to the church to mock the charakternyk. He cannot enter the church himself, so he asks you to go inside and bring back his saber in quest [[Stolen Saber]].
