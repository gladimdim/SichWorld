# 🧑 Khoma Riznyk

#npc

## Who
The butcher of the Ingulsk Palanka. Processes meat and hides for the palanka — cuts carcasses, salts meat, tans hides. Also goes hunting, though his shooting is mediocre — but he butchers game better than anyone.

## Character
Hot-headed, rough, loud. Speaks like he chops — briefly and sharply. Flares up easily but cools just as quickly. Not vindictive — may shout at you, then offer you meat an hour later. Cannot stand it when someone takes credit for another's work. Values strength and directness, despises cunning.

## Appearance
Big, broad-shouldered, hands thick as logs. Face red and weathered — from the sun or from anger, you can never tell. Apron always bloody. Large knife on his belt, which he uses both for cutting and for gesturing during conversation. Long mustache drooping downward. Shaves his head.

## Role
Stands by his butcher's shed with a cutting table at the edge of the palanka. Hides hang there drying as well.

## Connections
- [[🧑Opanas Kryvyi]] — quarreled over the catch. Used to get along fine, hunted together.
- [[🧑 💵Kozhem'yaka]] — delivers processed hides to him
- [[🧑Naum Lysenko. Ingulsk Kurinnyi]] — respects the Kurinnyi, but thinks he should take his side
