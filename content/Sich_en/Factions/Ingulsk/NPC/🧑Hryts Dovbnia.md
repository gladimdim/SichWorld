# 🧑 Hryts Dovbnia

#npc

## Who
Young Cossack, wants to become a hunter in Ingulsk, but for now an unlucky one. Recently came to the palanka, knows no one here — so he latches onto every new face.

## Character
Talkative, a bit cowardly, but sincere and kind-hearted. Wants to please everyone, which is why he often tries to make friends and pokes his nose where he isn't asked. Lies only out of fear, not malice. If someone believes in him and helps — Hryts will be loyal to the end. Has the potential to become a good hunter, but for now he lacks confidence more than skill.

## Appearance
Young, about twenty, still without a mustache — just fuzz. Thin, angular, long-armed. Moves clumsily, constantly bumping into things. Dressed poorly — old svytka (coat), worn belt, oversized boots. Old bow, bowstring tied with a knot. Big brown eyes with the expression of a dog that wants to be petted.

## Role
Wanders around the palanka. Stands now by [[🧑Khoma Riznyk|Khoma's]] shelter, now by [[💵🧑Honta|Honta's]] fire, now by the gate. Always tries to be useful, but more often gets in the way.

## Connections
- [[💵🧑Honta]] — wants Honta to take him as an apprentice, afraid to disappoint him
- [[🧑Khoma Riznyk]] — Khoma yells at him, but slips him food
- [[🧑Naum Lysenko. Ingulsk Kurinnyi]] — the kurinnyi is skeptical but gives him a chance
- Hero — the only person who can believe in him (or betray him)

## Character Arc
Hryts is a through-line NPC whose fate depends on the player:
- **Learned to shoot honestly** → helps in battle with mavky and [[Shapeshifter]], becomes a hunter in Ingulsk
- **Passed by deception** → remains an unlucky one, flees from battle
- **Betrayed to Honta** → offended, disappears from the palanka forever
