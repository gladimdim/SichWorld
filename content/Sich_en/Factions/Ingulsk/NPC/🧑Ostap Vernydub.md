# 🧑 Ostap Vernydub

#npc #charakternyk

## Who
An old Charakternyk who lives in a hut at the edge of the swamp north of the palanka. Brews rare potions that no one else in Ingulsk can make. Knowledgeable about otherworldly creatures and magic. Was once one of the best hunters in the palanka, but withdrew from people after some event he never speaks of.

## Character
Grumpy, blunt, distrustful of strangers. Speaks in riddles and hints, never directly. May curse you out and throw you out, or may suddenly help — you never know in advance. Dislikes fools and the impatient. But if he sees sincerity and intelligence in a person — he'll open up and become an invaluable ally. Behind the rough shell hides a man who knows too much and has become lonely because of it.

## Appearance
Old, dry as a branch, over sixty years old. White beard down to his waist, uncombed and with knots. Eyes sharp, light, piercing — seems to look right through you. Hands gnarled but nimble, constantly sorting through something — roots, herbs, bones. Dressed in a patched gray coat, with a necklace of animal bones and teeth. Smells of herbs and swamp. Walks with a staff of black willow, but more agile than he looks.

## Role
Lives in a hut near the swamp. Around the hut — drying herbs, bunches of roots hanging, pots with mysterious contents. People don't come to him without need — the Cossacks are wary. Thirty years ago he participated in a hunt for [[Shapeshifter]].

## Connections
- [[🧑 💵Pechyborshch]] — once taught him, but they quarreled. Considers Pechyborshch talented but lazy: *"Could have become a Charakternyk, but became a tavern keeper with bottles."*
- [[🧑Naum Lysenko. Ingulsk Kurinnyi]] — the Kurinnyi treats him with respect and leaves him alone. Ostap ignores palanka politics.
- [[💵🧑Honta]] — knew him when he was young, sometimes they exchange favors in silence
