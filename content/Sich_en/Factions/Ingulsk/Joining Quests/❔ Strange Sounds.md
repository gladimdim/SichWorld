# ❔ Strange Sounds
#join_quest
[[🧑Naum Lysenko. Ingulsk Kurinnyi]] complains that there are some sounds in the ravine and children are afraid to graze horses there. You need to go and find out what is happening.

On the way you kill simple mobs. Near the ravines a knocking can be heard. You approach and it turns out to be [[💵🧑Honta]] chopping trees. You ask him why he is making noise in the middle of nowhere. He says these trees are suitable for his new bow that he wants to make. He is looking for better wood but it's deeper in the ravine and he proposes to go together since he hasn't found the right branch yet. You go with him and he trains you a bit, you kill mobs, reach the place where he cuts his branches and you head off. For this you receive arrows and a simple bow.
