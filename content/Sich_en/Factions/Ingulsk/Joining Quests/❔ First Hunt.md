# ❔ First Hunt

#join_quest

## Quest Giver
[[🧑Naum Lysenko. Ingulsk Kurinnyi]]

## Description
After completing previous tasks [[🧑Naum Lysenko. Ingulsk Kurinnyi]] says:

*"Well then, you're not entirely hopeless. But in Ingulsk every Cossack must be a hunter. Words are not enough — show what you can do. Go to the steppe, bring me a saiga carcass. Not just any one — I need an old male with large horns. These aren't easy to find. Talk to the lads, maybe someone can point you in the right direction."*

## Quest Walkthrough

### Gathering Information
Naum hints that you should talk to other hunters. You can visit the palanka and question the NPCs:

**[[💵🧑Honta]]** — if you approach and ask, he will first wave you off: *"A saiga? Ha. I make bows, not chase steppe goats."* But if you insist, he advises: *"Old males keep separate from the herd. Don't look for a herd — look for individuals. They graze at dawn near ravines with water. And here's the thing — approach against the wind, because the saiga will smell you before it sees you."*

Honta gives **3 poisoned arrows** that slow prey: *"Here, take these. The saiga is faster than you — without these you won't catch it."*

**[[🧑 💵Pechyborshch]]** — if you visit him, he says: *"Going hunting? Here, take this then."* He gives **sharp vision potion** (for a time highlights animals in the grass). *"Just drink it before you go out to the steppe. It doesn't last long."*

**[[🧑Taras Chub]]** — if you already completed quest [[❔Wolf Attack]] and he trusts you, he says briefly: *"By the dry ravine to the east. Saw the horned one the day before yesterday. Big, old."* This gives **an exact map marker** — no need to search yourself.

If [[❔Wolf Attack]] wasn't done or Taras doesn't know you, he will only grumble: *"I don't know. Not my business."*

**[[🧑Hryts Dovbnia]]** — will run up himself when he sees you with a bow: *"You're going hunting?! Take me! I'll help! I've already shot... well, almost hit."*

You can take him with you. He won't hit the saiga, but during the hunt **he'll flush two rabbits from the grass**, which can be shot — additional loot.

### The Actual Hunt
You go out to the steppe. Depending on gathered information:

- **Have marker from Taras** → go straight to the dry ravine, find the saiga without wandering.
- **Have only Honta's advice** → need to search near ravines with water, check several locations before finding it.
- **Asked no one** → wander the steppe for a long time; wolves may attack along the way; eventually find the herd but not the old male. Find the male further on — separately.

When you found the saiga — you need to sneak up and shoot. The saiga flees after the first shot.

- **With Honta's poisoned arrows** → the saiga slows down, can be finished with a second shot.
- **With Pechyborshch's potion** → you see the saiga from a distance; the first shot from ideal position — better chance of killing outright.
- **Without preparation** → need to chase it and shoot on the run. Difficult, can miss and need to find another.

### Return
You bring the carcass to [[🧑Naum Lysenko. Ingulsk Kurinnyi|Naum]]. He examines the catch.

*"Good male. Old, horned — just right."*

If you took Hryts and he brought rabbits, Naum will smile: *"Ah, and Dovbnia was with you? Well, at least he learned to catch a rabbit, that's something."*

## Reward
- Naum Lysenko's respect (brings you closer to joining the palanka)
- Saiga horns (can be sold to [[🧑 💵Kozhem'yaka|Kozhem'yaka]] or [[🧑 💵Bosper|Bosper]])
- If you took Hryts — 2 rabbit skins (small bonus)
- If you did everything perfectly (spoke to everyone, killed with first shot) — Naum also gives a **hunting knife**: *"Here. A real hunter without a knife is no hunter."*
