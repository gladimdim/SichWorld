# ❔  Deer Antlers

#join_quest

[[🧑Naum Lysenko. Ingulsk Kurinnyi]] asks you to bring bear fur to prove there's some use in the hero for hunting.

The bear lives in a cave in a ravine. You can go there alone or ask [[💵🧑Honta]] for help. [[🧑 💵Pechyborshch]] before the quest, if you speak with him, can give a potion to help.

Near the deer are the remains of two apprentice Cossacks (dzury); take the better bow from them and a pile of arrows with potions. One of them will have a book [[Rare Animals]].
