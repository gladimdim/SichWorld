# ❔ Hunters' Dispute

#join_quest

## Quest Giver
[[🧑Naum Lysenko. Ingulsk Kurinnyi]]

## Description
You approach [[🧑Naum Lysenko. Ingulsk Kurinnyi|Naum]], and he sits by his kurin rubbing his temples:

*"Oh dear... Two fools have driven the whole palanka mad. Khoma Riznyk and Opanas Kryvyi — went hunting for a deer together, and now they can't divide the catch. Khoma says — he fired the arrow that stopped the deer. Opanas says — he finished it off and skinned it. Both are telling the truth, and both have been eating my head. Go sort it out, because I can't look at them anymore."*

## New NPCs

### Khoma Riznyk
A big, hot-tempered Cossack with a red face. A butcher by trade — processes meat and hides for the palanka. Blunt, straightforward, but not mean. Stands by his butcher's shelter with a cutting table.

### Opanas Kryvyi
A thin, sinewy hunter with a crooked nose (hence the nickname). Calmer than Khoma, but stubborn as a mule. Considers himself the better archer. Sits by the fire and pointedly sharpens his knife.

## Quest Walkthrough

### Investigation
You need to talk to both and listen to each.

**Khoma Riznyk** (at the butcher's shelter):
*"I shot first! The deer was galloping at full speed, and I hit it in the side. It fell, and that... Crooked-nose ran up and finished it off. And he says — his catch! Without my arrow he wouldn't have even seen that deer! The hide is mine. He can have the meat, I don't care, but the hide I skinned, I processed, and it's MINE."*

If asked about witnesses: *"Who was there? The steppe. The two of us. Well, Dovbnia was wandering somewhere around, but as usual he didn't see anything."*

**Opanas Kryvyi** (at the fire):
*"Khoma shot an arrow — that's true. But the arrow went in soft, the deer would have run on and died somewhere in a ravine where wolves would have eaten it. It was I who finished it off! I crept up and put a second arrow right in the neck. Without me — no hide, no meat. And Khoma skinned it — that's true, his hands are good for that. But the catch is mine because I killed it."*

If asked about witnesses, Opanas winces: *"Dovbnia? He was peeking from behind a bush. Maybe he saw something..."*

**[[🧑Hryts Dovbnia]]** — if you find him (he wanders around the palanka), he will initially get nervous:

*"Oh, I don't want to get between them... Khoma will wring my neck later..."*

If you press him, or if Hryts trusts you (from quest [[❔Unlucky]] or [[❔ First Hunt]]):

*"Alright, alright... I saw it. Khoma really did hit first, the deer limped. But it wouldn't have fallen — it would have run on. Opanas then ran ahead of the deer from the side and hit it in the neck. The deer ran a bit more and fell. Khoma was skinning it, because Opanas said it's not his style. But... I also saw one more thing. Before Khoma shot — the deer was already wounded. It had an old arrow sticking out of its leg. Someone had shot it earlier. The deer wouldn't have gotten far anyway."*

**[[💵🧑Honta]]** — if you show him the old arrow from the deer's leg (you need to first get it from Khoma or Opanas by asking to see the carcass), Honta will examine it and say:

*"This is [[🧑 💵 🏹Yatsko Lysytsia.|Yatsko Lysytsia's]] arrow. I recognize this fletching from a mile away — he's the only one who cuts feathers this way. So Yatsko was the first to shoot that deer, and your two heroes were just finishing off someone else's catch."*

## Resolution Options

### Option 1: Split Equally (without investigation)
Simply tell Naum to split the meat and hide equally. Naum shrugs: *"Could do it that way. But they'll hate each other anyway."*

Both Cossacks will be dissatisfied but comply with Naum. Minimal reward.

### Option 2: Take Sides
Decide that the catch belongs to Khoma (he shot first) or Opanas (he finished it off). One will be satisfied and give a gift; the other — resentful.

- **Khoma's side** → Khoma gives a piece of processed hide (can be sold)
- **Opanas's side** → Opanas gives several good-quality arrows

### Option 3: Reveal the Truth (requires investigation)
If you spoke with [[🧑Hryts Dovbnia|Hryts]], found the old arrow, and showed it to [[💵🧑Honta|Honta]] — you can gather everyone and announce:

*"The deer was already wounded by [[🧑 💵 🏹Yatsko Lysytsia.|Yatsko Lysytsia's]] arrow. He shot it first. You both were finishing off someone else's catch and still arguing over it."*

Khoma and Opanas freeze. Khoma mutters: *"Well, it's... so that's how it is."* Opanas scratches the back of his head: *"We didn't know..."*

[[🧑Naum Lysenko. Ingulsk Kurinnyi|Naum]] decides: the hide — to [[🧑 💵 🏹Yatsko Lysytsia.|Yatsko]] as the first hunter. The meat — split three ways. Khoma and Opanas reluctantly agree and even shake hands.

### Option 4: Duel (rough path)
During conversation with Khoma or Opanas choose aggressive dialogue (*"Enough arguing — let's settle it Cossack-style"*), you can provoke a fistfight between them. The winner takes all.

[[🧑Naum Lysenko. Ingulsk Kurinnyi|Naum]] will be displeased: *"I sent you to make peace, not start a fight..."* — less respect from the kurinnyi.

## Reward

| Option | Naum's Respect | Additionally |
|---|---|---|
| Split equally | Small | — |
| Take sides | Medium | Gift from the winner |
| Reveal the truth | Greatest | Both Khoma and Opanas are grateful, [[🧑 💵 🏹Yatsko Lysytsia.\|Yatsko]] finds out and gives a discount on his bows |
| Duel | Negative | Winner's meat |
