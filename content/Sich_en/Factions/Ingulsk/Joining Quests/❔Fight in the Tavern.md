# ❔ Fight in the Tavern

#join_quest

You approach the tavern, and two Cossacks are standing at the entrance harassing you and refusing to let you in. You can bribe them and enter, or beat one of them in a duel. Inside you'll find the healer [[🧑 💵Pechyborshch]], who is dead drunk on a bender. You ask him to return to his duties.
