# ❔ Finding a Doctor
#join_quest
When you bring the plants to [[🧑Naum Lysenko. Ingulsk Kurinnyi]], he gets angry that you brought them and sends you to the doctor who lives in a cottage in the palanka.

You arrive there, but he's not there — only his assistant, who says the doctor went to the tavern and has been there for two days.

You can give the medicine to the assistant, but then there will be little XP from [[🧑Naum Lysenko. Ingulsk Kurinnyi]].

Or go to the tavern and sort things out -> [[❔Fight in the Tavern]].

At the tavern you learn that [[🧑 💵Pechyborshch]] ate some mushrooms and needs to drink vodka for two days as an antidote. Today is the last day of drinking, and you can return together with him.
