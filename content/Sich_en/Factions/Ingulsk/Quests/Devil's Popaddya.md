The quest begins when you find an envelope with a curse and hair in the quest [[Drive the Djinn from the Cave]].

[[Popaddya Prokipivna]] is jealous of her husband [[Pip Prokip]] toward [[Little Cook]], because he constantly goes to her to eat, and she thinks it's not just eating. So she pulled a small lock of hair from the cook and cast a curse on it, then took it to the forest. The notes were picked up by chorty (demons) who bewitched [[Little Cook]], making her feel ill and barely able to leave the kitchen.

The hero picks up the envelope, which reveals this situation. The hero can go to [[Little Cook]] and ask about her health. She will say that for two weeks she's barely been able to drag herself around and can't work in the kitchen.

The hero can give her the enchanted hair and say [[Popaddya Prokipivna]] cursed her. The cook gets angry that the priest gorges himself like a pig and she was barely keeping up with cooking for everyone. So they can go to hell and she doesn't want to see them in her kitchen anymore. You receive XP as reward, and [[Little Cook]] gives you a bowl of borscht every day and also teaches you food recipes. That's the end of the quest.

The hero can go to [[Devil's Popaddya]] and say they found her charms, threatening to tell [[Pip Prokip]] and [[Little Cook]]. The Popaddya begs you not to expose her, and in exchange gives you money and a church amulet (a cross or holy water). She takes the charm envelope. After this the hero can go to [[Little Cook]] and tell her about the curse. She will confirm she is feeling better today, and that the priest's family is a strange one and they can both go far away from her. You receive some XP for the notification, but without the daily borscht.
