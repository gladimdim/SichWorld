# ❔ Curse

#quest #independent_quests #boss

## Quest Giver
[[🧑Ostap Vernydub]]

## Opening Conditions
- Befriend [[🧑Ostap Vernydub|Ostap]] in quest [[❔Swamp Healer]] (don't steal the root!)
- Know about the strange howling from quest [[❔Wolf Attack]]

If you stole the root or didn't do quest [[❔Wolf Attack]] — quest unavailable, [[Shapeshifter]] simply remains a danger on the map.

## Description
You return to [[🧑Ostap Vernydub|Ostap]] on the swamp. He sits by the fire and without greetings says:

*"It's a [[Shapeshifter]]. I knew he would return. Thirty years ago there was one just like him — barely drove him away with a group. You can't kill a Shapeshifter simply. The blade must be coated with poison from aconite and mavka bile. I can brew it, but I need the bile. And [[Mavky|mavky]] live on my very swamp... I'm not going to fight them."*

## Quest Walkthrough

### Stage 1 — Mavka Bile
You need to kill a [[Mavky|mavka]] on the swamp and bring the bile to [[🧑Ostap Vernydub|Ostap]]. A dangerous fight — mavky attack in groups.

Role of [[🧑Hryts Dovbnia|Hryts]] (depending on quest [[❔Unlucky]]):
- **Taught honestly** → Hryts comes on his own: *"I heard you're going after the Shapeshifter? I'm with you. I shoot better now!"* — becomes a companion, helps in the fight with mavky.
- **Gave fur (deception)** → Hryts comes too, but in battle misses and flees — no help.
- **Betrayed to Honta** → Hryts doesn't appear.

### Stage 2 — Poison
[[🧑Ostap Vernydub|Ostap]] brews the poison and coats your blade or arrows. Without this poison [[Shapeshifter|the Shapeshifter]] is nearly impossible to kill.

### Stage 3 — Hunting the Shapeshifter
[[Shapeshifter]] appears at night on the steppe between the forest and swamp. Two [[Wolf|wolves]] run with him.

Finding the Shapeshifter:
- **If [[🧑Taras Chub]] trusts you** (quest [[❔Wolf Attack]]): he'll say exactly where he saw the Shapeshifter: *"He comes to the watering hole when the moon is over the ravine. Wait for him there."* — exact location, easier to find.
- **If [[❔Wolf Attack]] wasn't done**: must search yourself, wandering the steppe at night, risking running into wolf packs.

Combat help:
- [[🧑Hryts Dovbnia]] (if taught honestly) — shoots from the flank
- [[💵🧑Honta|Honta]] can be called for extra pay
- With coated weapon — realistically killable at this level

## Reward
- **Shapeshifter hide** — rare material: [[🧑 💵Kozhem'yaka]] will buy for a huge sum, OR [[🧑 💵Bosper]] can make armor from it
- [[🧑Ostap Vernydub|Ostap]] becomes a permanent trader in rare potions
- [[🧑Naum Lysenko. Ingulsk Kurinnyi|Naum Lysenko]] greatly increases respect for the hero
- [[🧑Taras Chub]] smiles for the first time: *"Quiet now. Good."*
- If [[🧑Hryts Dovbnia|Hryts]] survived and helped — he is accepted into the Ingulsk hunters (his arc concludes)
