# ❔ Check the Traps

#independent_quests

## Stage 1: Check the Traps

[[🧑 💵 🏹Yatsko Lysytsia.]] asks you to check his traps in the forest — he doesn't have time himself, as he's working on a new bow. You need to check three traps, collect the catch, and bring it back to Yatsko.

- First trap — empty, but functional.
- Second trap — a rabbit in it. Take the catch.
- Third trap — broken, gnawed by wolves. Wolf tracks all around.

The player returns to Yatsko with the catch and tells him about the broken trap.

## Stage 2: Rope

Yatsko says the trap needs repairing, but he has no rope. He says to go to [[🧑 💵Kozhem'yaka]] in the palanka — he makes good leather straps to tie the trap.

**Option A:** If the player already completed the quest [[❔Attack on a Merchant]] — Kozhem'yaka will give the rope cheaply or for free, as he's grateful for the help.

**Option B:** If quest [[❔Attack on a Merchant]] was not completed — Kozhem'yaka doesn't trust a stranger and asks an inflated price for the rope.

## Stage 3: Fix the Trap

The player brings the rope to Yatsko. They go together to the broken trap and fix it. On the way Yatsko says wolves have been getting bolder lately and approaching the settlement ever closer.

Reward: hides, dried meat.
