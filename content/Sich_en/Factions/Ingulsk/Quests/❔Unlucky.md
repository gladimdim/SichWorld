# ❔ Unlucky

#quest #independent_quests

## Quest Giver
[[🧑Hryts Dovbnia]]

## Description
[[🧑Hryts Dovbnia]] pulls you aside: *"Listen, friend, help me out... [[💵🧑Honta]] gave me a trial — bring the fur of a wolf I killed myself. And I... well, you understand. I shoot like a blind man. Maybe you could bring me the fur? I'll say I killed it myself. Honta won't find out."*

## Resolution Options

### Option 1: Give the fur (deception)
If you have wolf fur (e.g. from quest [[❔Wolf Attack]]) — give it to Hryts. He's happy, takes it to [[💵🧑Honta|Honta]], is accepted. But Hryts remains a poor marksman — this will have consequences in quest [[❔Curse]].

### Option 2: Teach honestly
You refuse and offer to go hunting together. Hryts misses again and again; eventually he manages to shoot a [[Wolf|wolf]]. He earns the fur honestly. Takes more time, but Hryts **genuinely learns to shoot** — and this matters for quest [[❔Curse]].

### Option 3: Tell Honta
You go to [[💵🧑Honta|Honta]] and tell him about Hryts's plan. Honta gets angry and drives Hryts away. Hryts is offended and disappears from the palanka.

## Reward
- **Option 1:** Hryts is grateful but incompetent
- **Option 2:** Hryts becomes a reliable companion
- **Option 3:** [[💵🧑Honta]] gives arrows and some free training for honesty

## Connections to Other Quests
- Wolf fur can be obtained in quest [[❔Wolf Attack]]
- [[🧑Hryts Dovbnia|Hryts's]] fate determines his role in quest [[❔Curse]]:
  - Learned honestly → companion in battle
  - Deception → flees from battle
  - Betrayed → doesn't appear
