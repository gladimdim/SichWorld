# ❔ Wolf Attack

#quest #independent_quests

## Quest Giver
[[🧑Taras Chub]]

## Description
[[🧑Taras Chub]] stands near a pen with a mauled sheep. He says briefly: *"Wolves. The third one this week. Usually they don't come this close. Something is driving them here from the steppe. At least kill those near the pasture — I'll pay."*

## Quest Walkthrough
Go to the pasture and kill a pack of 4-5 [[Wolf|wolves]]. A straightforward fight.

When you search the killed wolves, you will notice that some have **deep gaping wounds on their backs** — not from humans and not from another wolf.

If you return to Taras and tell him about the wounds, he will grow glum: *"Something bigger than a wolf. I heard howling at night, but it wasn't a wolf's howl. I won't go there to check. And I don't advise you to either."*

## Reward
- Money
- Wolf hides
- [[🧑Taras Chub]] begins to trust the hero
