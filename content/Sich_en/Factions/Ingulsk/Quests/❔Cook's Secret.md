# ❔ Cook's Secret

#independent_quests

## Quest Giver
[[Little Cook]]

## Description

[[Little Cook]] approaches you and quietly asks for help. Tomorrow is a holiday — the anniversary of the palanka's founding — and [[🧑Naum Lysenko. Ingulsk Kurinnyi|Naum]] asked her to prepare a festive feast. But there's a problem: she wants to cook a legendary kulesh (millet porridge) from her grandmother's recipe, and she's missing two things — steppe honey and a special clay makhitra (cooking pot) in which kulesh turns out different from a regular cauldron.

*"I'm ashamed to ask the Cossacks — they'll say it's women's business. And the holiday is tomorrow, and I won't manage alone. Help me — I'll feed you so well that even [[💵🧑Honta|Honta]] will lick his fingers."*

## Quest Walkthrough

### Step 1: Steppe Honey

The cook says steppe honey is collected by an old beekeeper who lives in a ravine south of the palanka. But she doesn't remember exactly where.

You can ask:

**[[🧑 💵Pechyborshch]]** — he knows where the beekeeper is: *"Ah, Tykhon Medovyk? I know him. He brings me honey for tinctures. Lives past the second ravine, where the old willow grows. Just — he's deaf as a post — knock louder."*

**[[🧑Hryts Dovbnia]]** — if there's trust from previous quests, Hryts says: *"I know where he is! I can show you, I've got nothing to do anyway..."* — and goes with you as a companion.

You find the beekeeper **Tykhon Medovyk** — a deaf old man who keeps bees on steppe herbs. He's happy to have visitors and ready to give honey, but asks a small favor — help him move a heavy hive to a new location. A simple task requiring Strength or Agility.

After helping, Tykhon gives a pot of the finest steppe honey and says: *"Tell Little Cook to invite me to the feast. Been a while since I was among people."*

### Step 2: Clay Makhitra

The cook says the only person who makes such pots is a potter in Kodak. But she can't go there.

**Option A:** If [[🧑 💵Kozhem'yaka]] is in the palanka and trusts you (after quest [[❔Attack on a Merchant]]) — he says: *"Why go all the way to Kodak? I have one such pot, brought it from the fair. Here, I have no use for it."*

**Option B:** If Kozhem'yaka doesn't trust you — you'll have to buy the pot from him for 50 hryvnias.

**Option C:** You can go to [[🧑 💵Bosper|Bosper]] — he always has various odds and ends from merchants. Bosper will rummage around and find an old pot, but cracked. He says: *"The only one I have. If [[🧑 💵Pechyborshch|Pechyborshch]] seals the crack with his resin — it'll be as good as new."* You take it to Pechyborshch, who really does seal it, but takes a bunch of herbs from your inventory.

## Return

You bring the honey and pot to [[Little Cook|the cook]]. She is very happy:

*"Oh, thank you! Tomorrow the whole kurin will be stuffing themselves. Come first — I'll give you the biggest bowl!"*

## Reward

- Daily portion of kulesh from Little Cook (restores health)
- If you brought Tykhon Medovyk to the feast — he becomes accessible as a honey merchant near the palanka (once a week)
- Respect from [[🧑Naum Lysenko. Ingulsk Kurinnyi|Naum]]: *"I see you can help with more than just arrows. Good."*
- If Hryts helped — he receives praise from Naum for initiative (affects his arc in [[❔Unlucky]])
