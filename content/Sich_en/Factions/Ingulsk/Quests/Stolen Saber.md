Someone from the Cossacks stole the charakternyk [[Charakternyk Knyaz]]'s saber during a quarrel in the tavern and took it to the priest's house at the church. The charakternyk cannot speak with the priest or enter the church, so he asks you to return the saber.

You go to the church in the Palanka and ask the priest [[Pip Prokip]] about the saber. He says it serves the charakternyk right and it's a lesson for him. And he refuses to give you the saber.

You can buy it back from the priest for 1000 hryvnias.

You can threaten the priest (if Strength is 30) that not everyone makes it home from the tavern and the church won't protect from the demon that lives in [[Charakternyk Knyaz]].

If you have 30 Intelligence, you can say the saber is cursed and might defile the church, or even steal money from the church. Upon which [[Pip Prokip]] will ask you himself to return the saber to the charakternyk.

You can talk to the horse herder [[Cossack Konoval]]. Ask him what he knows about [[Charakternyk Knyaz]]. He will say he always helped him and drove demons away from the horses, especially at night. But lately for the past couple of days he hasn't seen the Charakternyk do anything, and one horse was dragged by demons into the swamp and drowned. You can say this is because someone stole his saber and gave it to the priest. [[Cossack Konoval]] gets very indignant and says that tonight he'll come and punch [[Pip Prokip]] in the face and burn that church, because he doesn't care about their quarrels when his horses are being stolen. With this information you return to [[Pip Prokip]] and pass on the threats. The priest is scared and gives you the saber. You remind him that a horse vanished because of their stupid prank and he also pays you 100 hryvnias to compensate [[Cossack Konoval]] for the horse.
