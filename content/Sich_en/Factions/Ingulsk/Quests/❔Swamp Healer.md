# ❔ Swamp Healer

#quest #independent_quests

## Quest Giver
[[🧑 💵Pechyborshch]]

## Description
[[🧑 💵Pechyborshch]] says: *"I need the root of a black willow that only grows in the swamp. I won't go there myself — my legs aren't what they used to be, and in the swamp... it's unclean. But without the root I can't brew a fever remedy. And three people in the palanka already have fever."*

## Quest Walkthrough
You go to the swamp. On the way you encounter [[Mavky]] (dangerous). You find the black willow, but near it stands the hut of [[🧑Ostap Vernydub|Ostap Vernydub]]. He comes out and shouts: *"Away from my tree! That's my root!"*

## Resolution Options

### Option 1: Ask
Ostap grumbles: *"What's in it for me? Pechyborshch has long forgotten the way here, and now sends youngsters? Bring me some beeswax from the wild apiary in the forest — then we'll talk."*

Mini-task: find the wild apiary in the relict forest, kill some bee-mobs, collect wax. Bring it to Ostap — he gives the root.

### Option 2: Steal
You can take the root while Ostap is in his hut. But then he becomes hostile and **won't help in the quest [[❔Curse]]**. [[🧑 💵Pechyborshch]] gets the root but says: *"He'll find out. He always finds out."*

### Option 3: Tell about the howling (requires [[❔Wolf Attack]])
If you know about the strange howling from quest [[❔Wolf Attack]], there is an additional option: *"Old man, something strange is happening in the steppe. Wolves are being driven out somehow, the howling at night is unearthly..."*

Ostap's face changes sharply: *"Howling, you say?.. Sit down. Tell me everything."* After the story he **gives the root himself** for free and says: *"Tell Pechyborshch he's a fool. And you... come back to me. I have something to tell you. But first do what needs to be done."*

## Reward
- [[🧑 💵Pechyborshch]] brews potions, heals the sick, gives several free potions
- If you befriended [[🧑Ostap Vernydub|Ostap]] — quest [[❔Curse]] opens

## Connections to Other Quests
- Quest [[❔Wolf Attack]] unlocks **Option 3** (the best one)
- Friendship with [[🧑Ostap Vernydub|Ostap]] — **required condition** to open quest [[❔Curse]]
- If you stole the root — quest [[❔Curse]] **blocked**
