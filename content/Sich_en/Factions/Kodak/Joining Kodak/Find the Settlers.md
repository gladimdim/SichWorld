# ❔ Find the Settlers

#join_quest

[[Ivanenko]] and Sokyrko went out on a ukhodnyky expedition a week ago, but neither has returned. Your task is to find them — they went north. You must follow the road and ask Cossacks you meet about these two. They will direct you further along. Eventually you venture far from the river and find their belongings. Nearby is a cave where the wounded Ivanenko lies. He asks you to heal him with a life potion (you can refuse). He asks you to help and check whether Sokyrko is still alive — an Upyr took him into the cave. During the night an Upyr attacked them, took Sokyrko, and wounded [[Ivanenko]]. You enter, fight through the [[Gaddia]], kill the Upyr, and find Sokyrko's saber (a nice one), which you bring back to [[Ostap. Kodak Kurinnyi]]. If you healed Ivanenko with a potion, he takes the saber for himself. If you didn't save him — he gives it to you, since you need it now.

Two days later he thanks you for snapping him out of his stupor and gives you a fine potion (or something of your choice).
