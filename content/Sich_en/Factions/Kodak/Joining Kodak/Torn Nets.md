[[Pylyp the Fisherman in Kodak]] — someone is tearing his nets. He complains to you about it. Says that at night someone comes and tears all his nets, and now he has trouble fishing. He asks you to deal with it.

After receiving the quest, you can ask other NPCs about the matter. If you walk near the nets in the evening, you'll see an NPC. You approach him. It's a vagrant. You threaten him, saying you'll beat him if he doesn't say what he's doing here. He'll say that [[Zhydov]], who owns the mill on the hill and the tavern, is making him tear the nets.

The hero goes to the tavern keeper and asks what's going on. He says that [[Pylyp the Fisherman in Kodak]] sold him rotten fish and poisoned his customers, causing him 200 gold in damages, and he even got beaten. So he's taking revenge on the fisherman, because the fisherman won't admit it.

You go to [[Pylyp the Fisherman in Kodak]] and ask about the incident. He says there really was such a conflict, and he didn't realize the tavern keeper had suffered so much because of it. So he asks you to bring him 100 gold as compensation, and that the next fish delivery will be free.

The hero goes to the tavern keeper [[Zhydov]], and there are two ending options:

- Say that the fisherman sincerely apologizes for the incident, and that he'll make the next delivery for free (hide the 100 gold)
- Say that the fisherman sincerely apologizes and passed along 100 gold (tell the truth)
- Say that the fisherman sincerely apologizes and passed along 200 gold (tell the truth + add your own 100 gold)

The first option gives less XP. The second option costs you 100 gold but gives 2x XP. The third option gives 4x XP.
