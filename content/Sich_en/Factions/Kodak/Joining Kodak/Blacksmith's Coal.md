#join_quest

The palanka has a blacksmith, [[Kovalenko]]. But his forge isn't working because coal deliveries to his furnace have stopped. He can't constantly go to the mine for coal himself. So he asks you to go to the mine and find out why coal isn't being brought to the palanka.

The mine is near the palanka's kurinnyi, through a small forest. Along the way you can kill various boars. The mine itself is empty — on a table in the camp there is only a note saying that something has moved into the mine and the miners refuse to work there.

You enter the mine, kill the cave bear. You can also loot spawns of coal and iron.

You return to the blacksmith and say you brought coal. He says it's enough for a week, but then he won't be able to work again. You need to find those miners near the palanka and tell them the beast is gone. You find all three of them.

If you spoke with the palanka's ataman [[Ostap. Kodak Kurinnyi]] beforehand, he will tell you to round up the miners and threaten them. If you didn't speak with him, you'll need to buy each one a beer.

After all miners return, you can complete the quest.
