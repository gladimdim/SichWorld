# ❔ Kill the Glutton

#join_quest

A giant otter has settled on the river and is blocking the fish from passing. Your task is to kill this creature — there will be plenty of loot, but it won't be easy to kill. The beast lives at a ford; smaller creatures guard the area around it. Behind the beast is its nest full of bones and loot from them.

When you approach the otter's nest, [[Ivanenko]] can help with the quest if you healed him in the [[Find the Settlers]] quest. If not — he died, and he won't be here.
