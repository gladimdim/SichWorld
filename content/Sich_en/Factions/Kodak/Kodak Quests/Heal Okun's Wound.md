The last battle with a Mavka cost [[Charakternyk Okun]] a deep cut on his side. The Mavka struck with her tail and infected the wound. He'll hold on and won't die on his own, but it will take a long time to recover. So he asks you to bring him healing herbs that grow on the riverbanks and small islands.

You gather several flowers, bring them to [[Charakternyk Okun]], and he asks you to also collect some old healing water from a well. The well is located in a dark forest patch where many [[Gaddia]] roam. You go there, kill the monsters around it, fill a bottle with water from the well.

When you bring the water back, [[Charakternyk Okun]] will brew a potion and recover. He will then offer you the treasure map [[Underwater Treasure Map]].
