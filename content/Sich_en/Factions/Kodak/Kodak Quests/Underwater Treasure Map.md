[[Charakternyk Okun]], after the [[Heal Okun's Wound]] quest is completed, will share an underwater treasure map with you.

You will need to come back to Okun each time to ask about the next treasure, once you've found the previous one.

First he tells you that his grandfather Shchuka, before leaving for the Don, left him a map of treasures hidden in various corners of Sich. The locations are given as riddles that must be solved. This is part of a test of worthiness.

## Akinak of Ares
Hidden at the bottom of the river where the Kamianka flows into the Borysfen.

## Helmet of Attila
Lies on an island in the middle of a lake.

## Mirror of Yaroslavna
Lies near the walls of Sich underwater.

## Bow of Hector
Also lies somewhere in the river.

When you bring all the artifacts to the Charakternyk, he grants you access to obtain [[Sviatoslav's Saber]].

Artifacts can be collected without the quest and brought to Okun afterward.
