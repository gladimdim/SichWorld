# 🏫 Kodak Palanka

[[Ostap. Kodak Kurinnyi]] is the Kurinnyi. The palanka specializes in fishing and excels in saber combat. There are trainers up to master level who will teach you.

[[Ostap. Kodak Kurinnyi]] handles recruits.

[[Main Quest Branch]]

## Specialties

Engages in fishing and boat building. Everything related to rivers and sea raids.

## Territory

A territory stretched along the Borysfen (Dnipro), rich in small rivers that flow into the Dnipro. Generally lowland, many gullies where rivers run, stone ridges occasionally protrude from the ground. Not suitable for farming. Between rivers and ridges — steppe and estuary.

## Military Affairs
Specializes in sabers. Produces fine sabers, and the kurinnyi specializes in saber training. This palanka has the best saber masters. Resources for saber making are exchanged with Samara Palanka.
