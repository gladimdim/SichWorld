# Name: Zhydov

# General
Tavern keeper in the Kodak Palanka. Has a conflict with [[Pylyp the Fisherman in Kodak]] over bad fish sold to him.

# Life Description
Inherited the tavern from his wife, whom he married for the tavern. The wife is also Jewish — this is her third husband. Has traded in vodka and run the tavern his whole life.

## Characteristics
Cheerful, greedy for money. Will do anything for money. Sells information for money.
