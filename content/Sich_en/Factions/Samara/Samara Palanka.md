# 🏫 Samara Palanka

Deals with resources — logging and hauling timber. Skilled with firearms; the best teachers up to the highest level are here.

[[Turchynov Samara Kurinnyi]] handles recruits.

[[Joining the Samara Palanka]]

## Specialties

Deals with forests and mineral extraction from the mountains. Manufactures firearms. The best firearms masters and gunpowder warriors live here.

## Territory

Located beyond the Samara River, which separates it from Kodak and Ingulsk. To the west are mountains with iron and coal. There are also oak forests beneath the mountains. The Samara River flows from a large lake to the north, between the Samara and Ingulsk Palankas.

## Military Affairs
Skilled with firearms, which they also manufacture here. The best gunsmiths and soldiers with muskets and cannons live here.
