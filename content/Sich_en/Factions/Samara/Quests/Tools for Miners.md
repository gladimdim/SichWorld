The quest starts with [[Andrii Krotulo]] when you find him. You need to pass information to [[Ivan Sirko. Ataman.]] that there are not enough tools for the miners.

After speaking with the Koshovy, he asks you to talk to the merchants about tool deliveries to the palanka. They will fleece you for 500 coins to draw up the contract.

Alternatively, you can go to [[Kovalenko]] in Kodak and ask if he could also produce tools for [[Samara Palanka]] in exchange for resources. He'll happily agree, as his small mine is already nearly empty. He won't charge you anything.

The merchant option gives little XP.

The blacksmith option gives more XP.
