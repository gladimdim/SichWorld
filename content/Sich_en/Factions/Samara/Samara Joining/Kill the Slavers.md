# ❔ Kill the Slavers

The quest is given either during the [[Joining the Samara Palanka]] quest chain, or is issued upon arriving at Sich.

You receive a scrap of paper with a rough sketch of the location of the cave where the slavers are hiding.

You can ask [[Tomash Maslo]] if he knows where his brother usually went hunting. He'll give you a direction — you go that way and find a cave entrance slightly off the path. You kill the guards at the entrance. Afterward you can return to [[Tomash Maslo]] and tell him you know where his brother is being held. He'll help you clear the cave of slavers.

At the end of the cave you free [[Andrii Maslo]], [[Tymko Koval]], and other missing people from other palankas.

They all return to their palankas, and when you meet them later they thank you.

[[Tymko Koval]] gives you a weapon of your choice that he managed to steal from somewhere.

You also receive a map showing the slavers' hidden treasures.
