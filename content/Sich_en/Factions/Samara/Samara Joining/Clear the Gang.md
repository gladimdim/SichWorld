# ❔ Clear the Gang

A gang nearby the palanka has been attacking lone Cossacks. You need to deal with them and find the missing [[Andrii Maslo]], who was supposed to handle this.

If you didn't turn in [[Tomash Maslo]] in the previous quest, he'll be able to help when you ask about his brother.

You attack the bandits' camp and kill everyone. Among the slain you find a note saying that captured Cossacks are being sold to Tatars. [[Andrii Maslo]] is being held by them. You also find a rough map draft showing the meeting place of the bandits and Tatars.

The quest [[Kill the Slavers]] begins.
