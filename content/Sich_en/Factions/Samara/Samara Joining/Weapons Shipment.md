# ❔ Weapons Shipment

[[Turchynov Samara Kurinnyi]] lets you know that since you're new here, you'll be able to find out who the rat in the palanka is. To do this, you need to find a missing shipment of muskets that was acquired during the last raid on Crimea.

After the return, someone stole several muskets.

You need to talk to various Cossacks about what anyone saw. This leads nowhere. But if you go to the tavern where a musket is for sale, the tavern keeper will say that [[Tomash Maslo]] frequently trades in goods of unknown origin.

To start a conversation with [[Tomash Maslo]] at the tavern, you first need to buy him a beer. He'll then agree to sell you a musket for a certain sum.

After the transaction, you can tell him he's been caught — because the musket is from the palanka's armory, and [[Turchynov Samara Kurinnyi]] is looking for the thief. [[Tomash Maslo]] will beg you not to turn him in, and in exchange will return the money. He also says he'll be able to obtain various things for you.

A choice arises: turn in [[Tomash Maslo]] or not.

If you turn him in, you get more XP, and he's thrown into a pit.

If you don't, he gives back the shipment of muskets, which you bring to [[Turchynov Samara Kurinnyi]] and say you found them near the wagons — they must have fallen off after the return and no one noticed. You get little XP because [[Turchynov Samara Kurinnyi]] doesn't believe it.
