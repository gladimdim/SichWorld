# ❔ Wet Gunpowder

One of the cellars keeps leaking and soaking the gunpowder, which must be hauled out and dried. You need to find this cellar in the settlement and ask [[Pushkarenko]] why it's leaking.

[[Pushkarenko]] will say there aren't enough planks to fix the cellar — he'd asked [[Vyaka]] to repair it, but Vyaka keeps ignoring the request.

[[Vyaka]] in turn says there aren't enough hands to get everything done, and tells [[Pushkarenko]] to go to hell. The hero offers to help. The side quest [[Collecting Wood]] begins.

After completing [[Collecting Wood]], [[Vyaka]] says he'll get it fixed.
